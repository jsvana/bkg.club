Four ways to make BKG readable on a phone without losing the MySpace

Every frame below is a real 375px phone width rendering of the site header, nav, and the INITIATION section, plus one bylaw as a body-copy sample. Swipe or scroll the row sideways to compare. The brass, the blood red, the marquee, the knuckles and the "ur" spelling stay in all of them.

What is actually breaking on phones today

Nav links go black-on-black

The header fades from brass to pure black. On a phone the twelve links wrap into three rows below the logo, and the bottom rows land on the black part. The text is black, 12px, and underlined.

Body copy is 12.8px light grey Comic Sans

Step descriptions, the members note and the bylaws are set at 0.8rem in #aaa. Comic Sans is also not installed on Android, so Android members get whatever the browser substitutes.

Floating knuckles sit on top of the text

Eight 60px emoji are pinned above the page at 60% opacity. On a wide screen they live in the margins. On a phone there are no margins, so they drift over paragraphs.

The INITIATION ribbon is wider than the box

It is set to never wrap with 3px letter-spacing and 30px side padding, so on narrow phones it overhangs the double border. The section's own 30px padding leaves about 290px for text.

How to approve

Reply with a letter. Mixing is fine, for example "B fonts with D's nav bar", since the nav bar, the knuckle fix, the ribbon fix and the padding are the same across A through D and can ship with any font choice.

  1. A is the no-argument option: nothing new to load, every fix is size, colour and layout.
  2. B is the recommendation if you want the Comic Sans look to survive on Android.
  3. C is the most "handwritten", so it needs the biggest size to stay readable.
  4. D is the most legible and the furthest from the current look.
